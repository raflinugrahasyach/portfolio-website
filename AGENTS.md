# UI/UX Design & Frontend Implementation Rules

For any UI/design work, frontend implementation, or visual overhaul, you MUST rigidly follow the rules provided in the local skills directories:
1. **Taste & Design System:** Refer to `./.agents/skills/ui-ux-kit/SKILL.md` to define the visual direction and generate/update the `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
2. **Anti-Slop Guardrails:** You MUST check your work against `./.agents/skills/no-slop-ui/SKILL.md` before finalizing any component. Avoid generic AI patterns (purple gradients, excessive glassmorphism, huge rounded corners).

Do not generate generic AI UI. Think like a top-tier enterprise designer.
